Work Experience Section (Deloitte & Liskara):
- The work experience section is really strong.
- Deloitte section: You should start with the outcome / number (don't be afraid to bold) then how you achieved it. 
  - Example: "Reduced workload by 30% for research analysis by redesigning prompts, streamlining workflows and presenting findings to board of directors leading to uptake of idea and implementation across 12-person team" (This is just an example, but hope you get the point).
- Have you got any numbers for the second bullet point (how much did you streamline efficiency by?)
